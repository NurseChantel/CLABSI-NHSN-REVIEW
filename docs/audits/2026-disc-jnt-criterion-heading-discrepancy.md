# DISC and JNT criterion heading presentation discrepancy

## Source review and scope

This presentation audit uses `Secondary BSI Chapter.pdf`, Chapter 17, DISC,
printed page 17-8 (PDF page 9), and JNT, printed page 17-9 (PDF page 10).
The manual presents DISC criterion 3b and JNT criterion 3d once each, with
definitive imaging and clinically correlated equivocal imaging as alternatives
within the applicable criterion.

## Discrepancy report

The application evaluator represents each imaging alternative as a separate
branch so its distinct AND requirements remain explicit. The criterion-centered
BJ interface rendered those internal branches as separate top-level accordions.
Consequently, DISC displayed two headings labelled criterion 3b and JNT
displayed two headings labelled criterion 3d.

This is a presentation duplicate. The underlying definitive and equivocal
branches, their evidence requirements, and their NHSN source metadata are
supported and must remain distinct for evaluation.

## Authorized correction

Keep the evaluator branches and source metadata unchanged. Present DISC
criterion 3b and JNT criterion 3d once each, nesting their definitive and
equivocal imaging branches as explicit alternatives under the single applicable
criterion heading. Preserve the evidence controls, branch statuses, and
evaluation results for both alternatives.
